Who We Are

Steppers Academy is a premier coaching institute dedicated to academic excellence and competitive exam preparation. Located at The Stepping Stone High School near Javed Sabzi Mandiii, we serve students from Classes 6 through 10 with specialized, structured coaching programs.

Our team of six highly qualified mentors brings years of experience in teaching, competitive exam coaching, and student mentorship. We believe every student deserves the best possible preparation to achieve their dreams.

  • Expert & Qualified Teaching Faculty
  • CCTV Monitored Safe Campus
  • Solar-Powered Uninterrupted Classes
  • Monthly Assessment & Progress Reports
  • Comprehensive Syllabus Coverage
  • IBA, NTS, UniSindh Exam Experts
🎯

Our Mission

To provide quality education and competitive exam coaching that empowers students to achieve academic excellence and pursue their aspirations with confidence.

🔭

Our Vision

To be the most trusted and results-driven coaching academy in Sindh, known for building well-rounded, intellectually strong, and career-ready individuals.

Sir Veer Sanjot
Junior Subject Teacher (JST) — Steppers Academy

Sir Veer is a passionate educator and core mentor at Steppers Academy. His dedication to student development goes beyond textbooks — he focuses on building critical thinking, confidence, and a love for learning in every student he teaches.
